Who operates this site

This website is published by Museum Planning LLC, a United States–based museum planning practice. The site’s primary audience is institutions and colleagues evaluating feasibility, strategic planning, master planning, and exhibition work.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic — for example which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are located (city / region level), devices and browsers in broad categories, and how people move through the site.

Google processes this information according to its own policies. You can read how Google uses data from partner sites at Google’s partner sites policy.

Cookies & similar technologies

Analytics may place small text files (“cookies”) or use similar storage so visits can be distinguished from one another and counted reliably. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Forms & email

When you use contact forms on this site, your submission is typically sent through your email application using a mailto: link — we do not operate a separate inbox database on this static site for those messages. Newsletter signup flows may route through your email client or a third-party provider configured elsewhere; see any signup screen linked from those flows for specifics.

Retention & changes

Analytics data retention follows settings configured in your Google Analytics property. We may update this page occasionally as the site or laws evolve.
