Where we work commercially

The path is direct: feasibility study → city sponsor → master plan. The conversation almost never starts with "we want a museum" — downtown revitalization, Chamber foot traffic, workforce/tech goals, or a developer adding an educational anchor to a mall or housing project — and then someone names a museum, science center, or children's museum. City manager's staff searches the web and emails a short list. That is who these pages are for.
