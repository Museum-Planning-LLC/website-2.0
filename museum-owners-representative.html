FAQ

Owner’s Rep — common questions

What does a museum owner’s representative do?
Sit on the institution’s side during design, construction, and fabrication — reviewing bids, change orders, schedules, and quality so the museum’s program and budget stay protected.
When should we hire an owner’s rep?
After the plan and building program are clear — typically as architects, contractors, or fabricators are selected — and before major capital is committed.
How much does it cost?
Variable by scope and duration. Building-only, exhibition fabrication-only, or combined engagements are scoped after a one-hour conversation at no cost.
