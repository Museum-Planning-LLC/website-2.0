Natural History Museum

Mark August 19, 2025Natural History Museum

Role: Master Planning, Exhibition Design, Strategic Planning
Date: October 2024

Museum Master Plan PDF — October 2024 (25 pages: building program, architecture, floor plans, renderings, schedule, budget)

Project Overview

The Natural History Museum is a transformative cultural destination planned for Riverside County, California. Drawing from the extensive collection of a conservationist, the museum will showcase over 10,000 specimens through immersive exhibits, interactive programming, and community-centered experiences.

Design and Building Program

  • 23,000 sq. ft. facility on a 7-acre site adjacent to I-10, ensuring high visibility.
  • Landmark architectural features, including a light-filled clerestory and entry experience.
  • Integration of indoor exhibits with interpretive walking trails and outdoor spaces.
  • Conceptual architecture and site plans developed by Museum Planning LLC

Exhibits and Visitor Experience

  • Over 1,500 rotating nature specimens on display.
  • State-of-the-art interactive multimedia installations.
  • Hands-on STEAM programs incorporating AR/VR.
  • Family-oriented programming, live demonstrations, and citizen science initiatives.

Educational and Community Programming

  • K–12 field trips aligned with Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS).
  • Workshops and after-school classes on ecosystems, wildlife habitats, and climate change.
  • Public lecture series, family science days, and conservation-driven community outreach.
  • Inclusive programs including multilingual materials and sensory-friendly experiences for visitors with disabilities.

Timeline and Budget

  • Opening planned for 2027.
  • Phased implementation: planning & design (2023–24), site preparation & construction (2024–25), exhibit installation & programming (2025–26).
  • Capital budget: $27.75M
