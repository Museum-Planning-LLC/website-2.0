# Site strategy & funnel (internal reference)

**Audience (priority order)**  
1. **Cities & municipalities** — city manager, mayor’s office, economic development, council-related decision paths; often triggered by a **historic civic building** or downtown/cultural anchor conversation.  
2. **Science & technology centers** — civic-scale institutions where workforce, employer signal, and operating reality matter (not small retail “attractions”).  
3. **Universities & established institutions** — deans, boards, advancement; feasibility and master planning before capital.  
4. **Breadth** — Museum School and core service pages support generic “starting a museum” and consultant intent without duplicating city-specific pages.

**Non-priority (qualify out in copy and fees)**  
One-off strip-mall or hobby “museum” concepts with no institutional path; children’s museum segment is **portfolio credibility**, not primary hero positioning.

## How people find us (typical paths)

- **Search** — Problem-led queries after a meeting (e.g. museum consultant, feasibility study, starting a museum / science center, city museum, local history museum, historic building museum).  
- **Referral** — Colleagues, architect/engineer, peer institutions.  
- **Direct** — Prior studies, articles, speaking, repeat clients.

## Funnel (website role)

1. **Land** on a page that matches intent (home, **For Cities**, **Science Centers**, Services, Museum School article, project page).  
2. **Trust** — methodology, projects, tone, concrete fees/ranges, public-sector language.  
3. **Convert** — **Start a Conversation** → contact; municipal pages also use **mailto** forms as a lightweight capture.  
4. **Close (off-site)** — scoping call → proposal → **feasibility study** (often first paid gate) → master plan / exhibitions / campaign support as appropriate.

**Principle:** Feasibility and clarity on *what* is being built and *whether it can operate* precede major campaign and design commitments.
