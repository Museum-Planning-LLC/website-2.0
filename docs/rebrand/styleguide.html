01 — Overview

Four properties. One system.

Each property has a distinct identity and audience, but all share common foundations: the same sans and mono typefaces, a shared spacing and layout system, and consistent component patterns. The serif typeface and accent color are the primary differentiators between sites.

Serif Split — Design Rationale

Playfair Display is used for museumplanning.com and museums101.com — the consulting and institutional-facing properties. It is authoritative, high-weight, and carries institutional credibility appropriate for a $100K practice. Cormorant Garamond is used for museum-experiences.com and markwalhimer.com — the book companion and art practice. It is literary, refined, and carries the weight of a practiced author and artist. The split is intentional and should be maintained.

Usage Rules

Backgrounds: Always light (cream or off-white). Never pure white (#fff) on museumplanning.com or museums101 — use the warm off-white variants instead.

Dark sections: Permitted as accent only. One per page maximum (excluding footer). Use --navy/#1B2A3B for mp.com, --ink for museums101.

Accent colors: Gold for mp.com, Red for museums101, Copper for museum-exp. Never mix accent colors between sites.

markwalhimer.com: No accent color. Neutral ink only. The art speaks — no color competition.

05 — Spacing

Consistent spacing scale.

All four sites use a consistent spacing system. Section padding is 80–100px vertical, 48–56px horizontal. Max content width is 1100–1140px centered.

Layout Rules
Max content width
1100–1140px centered. Use max-width + margin: 0 auto on inner containers.
Card gaps
2px gap between cards (not 16–24px). Creates grid-line effect, feels architectural.
Grid columns
2-col and 3-col grids. Never 4-col on main content. Use 4-col only for stat/proof bands.
06 — Site Rules

What each site does and doesn't do.

Clear separation of concerns. Each site has one job. These rules prevent content creep and maintain brand clarity.

museumplanning.com
✓ Does: Consulting services, methodology, project case studies, MVI framework, Convergence Era white paper, contact/hire form, fee signals, newsletter.

✗ Does not: Art portfolio, teaching CV, book sales (links to books101.com), domain-level content marketing, museum school (keep minimal).
museums101.com
✓ Does: Book content, TOC, resources, toolbox, second edition teaser, links to consulting. Lead generation for museumplanning.com.

✗ Does not: Consulting pitch, art content, course sales (link to museumcourses.com).
museum-experiences.com
✓ Does: Book two content, visitor experience resources, templates, author bio linking to museumplanning.com. Remove AdSense. Remove "(needs updating)" notes.

✗ Does not: Consulting pitch, teaching résumé, art content.
markwalhimer.com
✓ Does: Art portfolio, selected works, thesis, commissioning pathway ("Available for institutional commissions and private collections"), one-line bio with consulting link.

✗ Does not: Teaching CV, résumé, book sales, consulting pitch, blockchain origin story.
