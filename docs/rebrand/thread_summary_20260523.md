## The MVI (Museum Vitality Index)

A proprietary scoring framework developed by Museum Planning LLC. Evaluates museum performance across five dimensions using size-normalized metrics — never raw numbers.

**Five categories (20 points each):**
1. Visitor Experience
2. Digital Presence
3. Financial Health
4. Community Impact
5. Institutional Strength

**Score scale:** 90–100 = World Class · 80–89 = Exceptional · 70–79 = Strong · 60–69 = Developing · Below 50 = At Risk

**Two deliverables per assessment:**
- Full Report (18–25 sections, internal verification document)
- Executive Summary (5–6 pages, client-facing)

**Example:** Connecticut River Museum scored 79 (B+ / Strong). Mystic Seaport benchmark: 88.

The MVI is a business development tool as well as a client deliverable — it positions Mark as the person who invented the measurement framework for the field.
