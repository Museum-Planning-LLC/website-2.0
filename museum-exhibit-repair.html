For mayors, city council members and museum directors

Your city or county museum has broken exhibits. Visitors see "Out of Order" signs. Staff can't fix them, the company that built them is gone, and every vendor wants to sell you new hardware. We fix broken museum exhibits and get them working again, on a clear budget and a clear timeline.

We fix the exhibit, not just the hardware

Most companies will sell you new screens and media players and put your old content back on them. We get your broken exhibits working again with new content and new interactives. We write the scripts, build the interactives, and work with local video producers and local IT installers who meet your city or county requirements.

Who we work with

Museum directors, mayors, city council members, city managers and county administrators. City and county museums, history museums, children's museums, science centers, visitor centers and national park sites.

Broken exhibits, museum exhibit repair and exhibit modernization

If your museum has broken exhibits, we can help. Many museums have not been able to fix broken exhibits or update outdated museum exhibits since before COVID. Touchscreens stop responding, kiosks freeze, and legacy exhibit software, including old Flash interactives, no longer runs.

What we provide:

  • Interactive exhibit repair and exhibit AV repair
  • Museum touchscreen repair and museum kiosk repair
  • Exhibit refurbishment and exhibit modernization
  • Interactive exhibit upgrades and PC migration, rebuilding old interactives on current, supportable software
  • Ongoing museum exhibit maintenance so your exhibits keep working

Hardware and platforms we work with: BrightSign, Q-SYS, Crestron, Linux media players, Mac mini, touchscreen calibration, RFID triggers and exhibit AV troubleshooting.

From science center exhibit repair and children's museum exhibit repair to visitor center exhibit repair, we fix broken museum exhibits no matter who originally built them.
