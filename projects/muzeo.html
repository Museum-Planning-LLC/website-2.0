Project Overview

Museum Planning LLC developed master planning and strategic planning for Muzeo in Anaheim, California — a community museum dedicated to the history and culture of Southern California. The work helped position Muzeo as a vital community resource and a destination for regional history and traveling exhibitions.

Master Planning

The master plan addressed the museum's physical spaces, visitor experience, and long-term development strategy — aligning the facility with the institution's community mission and growth ambitions.

Strategic Planning

A multi-year strategic plan was developed to guide programming, audience development, partnerships, and financial sustainability — giving the institution a clear roadmap for the years ahead.
