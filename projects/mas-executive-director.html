Rebuilding for
the Future

The Museum of Arts and Sciences had long served as a traditional regional institution — beloved by the Macon community, but in need of bold restructuring to evolve into a vibrant, future-facing organization. Mark Walhimer was engaged as Executive Director to lead that transformation from the inside out.

The work began with fundamentals: understanding the institution's collections, its people, and its community. From there, a comprehensive framework was built — staffing structure, reporting relationships, job descriptions, time and accountability systems, and a vision for how the museum's physical spaces, especially the Discovery House, could be reimagined for a new era.

This page covers the institutional and operational dimension of the work. The second part of this portfolio entry covers the transformative Art & Tech collaboration program that emerged in parallel — an initiative that became the museum's most innovative public programming effort in years.

Discovery House

Planning a Space
for the Future

Central to the institutional vision was the renovation and reimagining of Discovery House — the museum's primary interactive learning space and the anchor for its STEAM education programming.

Planning began with understanding how the space was being used, how it could be transformed, and how it could become a model for the kind of cross-disciplinary, community-centered experience that the museum was becoming.
